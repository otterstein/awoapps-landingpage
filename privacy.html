2. Platform & Requirements

This app is exclusively developed for Android (Android 9.0 Pie / API level 28 or higher). No data processing occurs via other platforms such as iOS or web.

3. Local Data Storage

The app saves your settings (e.g., time controls, increments, sound preferences) exclusively on your local device. No user profiles or match histories are transmitted to external servers.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob & UMP)

Third-Party Service

To fund the free availability of this app, we use Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

AdMob may process the following technical data:

  • Anonymized or pseudonymized device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address (anonymized/truncated)
  • Technical device specifications (Android OS version, device model)
  • Interaction data with displayed ads

Consent Management (Google UMP SDK): On your first launch in regions subject to privacy regulations (such as EEA/GDPR), a consent form powered by Google UMP SDK is shown. You can choose between personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or manage your preferences. You can adjust or revoke your consent anytime within the app settings.

For details, review: Google Privacy Policy.

5. User Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, or delete your data. Because no personal data is stored on external servers, you can erase local data yourself by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.
